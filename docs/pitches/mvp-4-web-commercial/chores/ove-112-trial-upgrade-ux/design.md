# OVE-112 — Trial upgrade UX, trial notices & BLIZZY copy

A focused chore on top of Plans, Billing & Promo Codes (Pitch 19). It reuses that pitch's entitlement model, hosted Checkout, Customer Portal, and verified webhook. It adds no new billing system. The visual contract is `preview.tsx` in this folder.

## What changes for the lifter

| Surface                     | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plan & Billing              | "Subscribe to Pro" section. Choosing Monthly or Annual shows a disclosure first: the selected price, and for a trial account that subscribing now starts paid billing today. "Continue to checkout" then goes to Stripe. Leaving Stripe without paying changes nothing.                                  |
| Trial notice, one day left  | One dialog on the next authenticated load when the trial ends tomorrow (or today, for a first visit on the last day) on the account's own calendar. **Keep Pro** links to Plan & Billing; **Maybe later** dismisses.                                                                                     |
| Trial notice, trial ended   | One dialog on the next authenticated load after the trial's authoritative end, when the account is on Free. **Upgrade to Pro** links to Plan & Billing; **Continue with Free** dismisses.                                                                                                               |
| Pricing page                | The "Is there a discount code?" FAQ is gone. BLIZZY appears on no generic page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `/blizzy`                   | 25% off Pro for three months, up to 200 redemptions. On monthly, that is the first three monthly payments. On annual, it is the first annual payment only, and renewals are at the regular price. Not monthly-only.                                                                                       |
| App shell (iOS, once built) | Plan & Billing is read-only (no Subscribe, no Manage billing). The notices keep their facts, drop every purchase sentence and action, and offer one "Got it".                                                                                                                                             |

### Added after review: closing the two-tab gap

| #    | Decision | Rationale | Authority |
| ---- | -------- | --------- | --------- |
| D-15 | The webhook mirror keeps the account's first **established** subscription (active, trialing, past_due, unpaid, paused). Events for any other subscription are ignored while that one is still established, which is re-read from Stripe so a stale mirror never blocks a genuine resubscription. A per-customer row lock serializes events. | Without this, a duplicate's events would make the mirror flip between two subscriptions, and its later cancellation could show a paying lifter as Free. | Owner follow-up on PR #153 |
| D-16 | After `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.created`/`updated`, and `invoice.paid`, `enforceSingleSubscription` (1) expires leftover open Checkout sessions and (2) for any other open subscription, refunds its paid invoice in full (`reason: duplicate`, idempotency key per payment) and **then** cancels it (no proration, no final invoice). It runs on deduped replays too; a failure returns 500 so Stripe retries. | Turns "two tabs both paid" into one subscription and an automatic full refund, with no owner action. Refunding before cancelling means a mid-way failure is retried, not left as a charged-but-cancelled duplicate. | Owner follow-up on PR #153 |
| D-17 | Recommend, not require: turn on Stripe's "Limit customers to one subscription" Checkout setting in test and live (runbook §2). | Defence in depth on Stripe's side. Stripe doesn't document whether it re-checks at payment time, so the webhook guard stays the guarantee. | Stripe docs (`/payments/checkout/limit-subscriptions`) |

## Platform impact

Shared web UI. The app shell gets the read-only variant through the single user-agent predicate (D-10). No native code, no native stub, and no dependency on the unfinished iOS branch. The shell behavior is **unverified** until iOS Foundation appends the token and runs on a device.

## Plan placement

No new capability. Subscribing is available to Free and trial accounts on the web. The notices are shown to trial and post-trial Free accounts only.
