Design handoff · v1.0

A week on one screen, a session in one hand, nothing filled in that he didn't type.

Pitch 3 lets the lifter author a training block: setup with editable 5-week / week-5 defaults, a structural preset or a blank week, sessions built from the Exercise Library with planned sets and rep ranges, day assignment, duplication, clone-forward, and repair of archived exercises. Nothing is logged, weighed, or tagged yet. Check every frame against one commitment: Overload arranges and checks the plan; it never writes it.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The app is built with Tailwind and shadcn/ui against the theme in globals.css; nothing here is copied into it. Mock data only.

What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments to check the frames above against.

The lifter authors the plan

  • Structure, never prescription. Presets create day names only. No preset, default, or empty state fills in an exercise, set count, rep range, or weight; the plan dialog starts empty.
  • Defaults are pre-fills. 5 weeks and deload week 5 are editable. Other lengths and non-final deload weeks are ordinary; a deload past the end is shown, not moved.
  • Copies are copies. Duplicates and clones are named copies the lifter lands on, with no visible link back to their source.
  • Nothing is quietly repaired. An archived exercise keeps its slot, marked with a dashed border and an Archived badge, until the lifter replaces or removes it.

Draft freely, activate deliberately

  • One gate. Drafts save half-built. Activate stays unavailable until the checklist is empty, and it is the only step that confirms.
  • One session per day, one active block. Occupied days always ask; activating archives the previous block in the same step.
  • Removal is authoring. Removing a session or a planned exercise never touches the Exercise Library, gyms, or other mesocycles, and the copy says so.
  • Quiet and thumb-first. No tags, no progress or deload color, no drag-only controls. Primary actions sit at the bottom; every target is 44px or more.
