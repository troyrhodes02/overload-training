Appearance
Design handoff · v1.0

Point at the real thing, say one sentence, step aside.

A new account's first visit opens a short tour over the real interface — Today, logging, Plan, History, Goals, Gyms, and Settings — with a spotlight on an actual control and one card that says what the area is for. It can be skipped at any step, never opens by itself again, and Settings replays it. Check every frame against "does this point at something the app really renders, at this width, for this account?" Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The app is built with Tailwind and shadcn/ui against the theme in globals.css; nothing here is copied into it. Mock data only.

What decides the first frameruleeligibility
  • Never offered → opens by itself. A brand-new account, on its first authenticated page. A deep link lands there first; step 1 then takes the lifter to Today.
  • Offered once, ever. The offer is spent when the tour first appears — finish, skip, Escape, browser Back, closing the tab, or reloading all leave it spent.
  • Accounts from before the tour are treated as already offered (standing resolution). They replay it from Settings.
  • Replays never re-arm it. However a replay ends, the tour does not open by itself again.

Missing target — skippedruleAC-3 · no message
  • Desktop: step 3 (menu button) is not offered — the counter reads 1–7 of 7, and Next on step 2 goes straight to Plan.
  • Absent, hidden, or inside a dimmed (inert) region = not there. The step is skipped in the direction of travel, silently; the total drops by one.
  • A screen that fails to load has none of its targets, so its step is skipped after a short wait; the tour carries on.
  • Last step missing → the tour ends as finished. Nothing showable → it closes and records nothing.
  • Resizing across md re-picks the target (rail item ↔ heading); a phone-only step showing at that moment moves on.

What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

The tour

  • Real controls only. Every frame spotlights an element the app already renders. No screenshots, no sample data, no fake workout.
  • Explain, never require. Next, Back, Skip, Done — nothing else. The highlighted control cannot be pressed during the tour.
  • Honest about an empty account. Copy says what an area is for; step 2 changes its words when no workout exists.
  • One tour, every width. First visible target wins — rail item at md+, heading or menu button below; the sheet stays closed.
  • Missing means skipped. Silently, in the direction of travel; the tour never breaks or points at nothing.

The product

  • Once on its own. A new account's first visit; never again after finish, skip, or abandon. Pre-existing accounts are not toured.
  • Any time from Settings. Replay tour, one outline button, same tour, never re-arms the automatic offer.
  • Native, quiet. shadcn Dialog, Button, Skeleton; popover surface, accent ring, dialog scrim — no new color, no tour library.
  • Thumb first on phones. The card docks at the bottom with 44px buttons; on desktop it sits beside its target.
  • Nothing left behind. No badges, checklists, help bubbles, or success toast; outcome is private to the account.
