Design handoff · v1.0

Say what changed, once, then keep it on the shelf.

When a significant update ships, the next load shows one calm dialog listing every significant update the account hasn't seen, and it never shows those again. Settings → What's New keeps every entry, newest first. Nothing is fabricated: the page launches empty, and new accounts start caught up. Check every frame against “could this interrupt the same lifter twice, or show them something about anyone but the product?” Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The app is built with Tailwind and shadcn/ui against the theme in globals.css; nothing here is copied into it. Entries are illustrative mock data — none ship with this pitch.

Behavior commitments

  • Once. Seen is recorded when the dialog opens; no reload, sign-in, or device shows it again.
  • Together. Any number of unseen significant entries share one dialog, newest first.
  • Significant only. Ordinary entries never interrupt; they are on the page.
  • No backlog. A new account starts caught up; the tour, not this dialog, owns its first visit.
  • Nothing fabricated. No historical or backdated entries; the page launches empty.

Visual commitments

  • The app's own dialog. Centered stock DialogContent at every width; header and Got it fixed, list scrolls.
  • Thumb reach. Got it is full width and 44px on phones, first in the stack.
  • No read state on screen. No unread dot, count badge, or “New” label in Settings or on the page.
  • One entry block. Date · title · body, identical in the dialog, on the page, and later in public.
  • Quiet. No promotion, no CTA inside an entry, no icon that reads as marketing; stock tokens only.
