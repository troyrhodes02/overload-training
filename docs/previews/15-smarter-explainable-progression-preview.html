Design handoff · v1.0

Say what to do, then show the receipt.

Every call the progression engine makes keeps its tag and gains two lines: an action (try this weight, stay here, lower the weight) and one line of evidence naming the rule that fired, in the lifter's own numbers. Nothing new is computed: weights appear only where the engine already has one, RIR only where it changed the call, and ordinary holds stay untagged and muted. Check every frame against one rule: the words can never disagree with the call. Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

A rendering of the design for review — not product code. The app is built with Tailwind and shadcn/ui against globals.css.

What these screens are built to respect

The commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

The engine's words, not the interface's

  • Every line comes from the one evaluation that produced the tag; nothing on screen is a second calculation.
  • A number only where the engine has one: +5 on a known working weight, and the deload week's 90%. Reactive Deload and bodyweight never get a weight.
  • RIR is named only when it changed the call (cases A and B). Unrecorded is never 0 and never 2+.
  • Machines and cables are judged per gym and the evidence says which gym.
  • No fatigue, recovery, or certainty words. Try, Stay, Lower — advice, never a promise.

Quiet, and nothing changes but the words

  • Hold still has no tag and no colour; its two lines are muted.
  • Two deloads, two voices: a deload week never shows a reactive tag or reactive words.
  • Nothing to tap. The lines are text; the weight field's offer stays the working weight; the plan and every stored weight are untouched.
  • Same card, same taps: 18 taps / 18 keystrokes, no new focus stop.
  • No new screen, chart, or history view; light and near-black dark via tokens only.
